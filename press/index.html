About Nova Guide

In 50 words

Nova Guide is an iPhone app for busy, overloaded students. It turns assignments, classes, and to-dos into one calm plan for today, with short study blocks, built-in breaks, and a plain-language reason for each suggestion. There are no accounts and no servers: a student’s data stays on their iPhone.

In 100 words

Nova Guide helps students decide what to do, when to do it, and how to begin. School days pile up fast; Nova Guide turns assignments, classes, quizzes, and to-dos into one calm, ordered plan for today, with short study blocks, built-in breaks, and a plain-language reason for every step. Assignments can be added by hand or imported from Google Classroom; classes come from Google Calendar, the iPhone Calendar, or a repeating entry typed once. Optional Lock Screen study sessions keep the next step in view. Nova Guide has no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no tracking — everything stays on the student’s device. It is an organizational and educational tool, not a medical or therapeutic product.

Fast facts

  • Created by Isla Hughes, Ipswich High School, Ipswich, Massachusetts
  • Company Nova Guide LLC, Massachusetts
  • Platform iPhone, iOS 26 or later
  • Price Free to download on the App Store, with an optional Nova Guide Plus subscription
  • Audience U.S. high-school and college students
  • Accounts None needed; works offline from the first launch
  • Google Classroom and Calendar connections are optional and read-only
  • AI On supported iPhones, Plus uses Apple’s on-device intelligence to write study steps and tips, and nothing is sent to an outside AI service. Free includes built-in steps by subject
  • Privacy No Nova Guide servers, no ads, no tracking tools of our own
  • Name Please write it as two words: Nova Guide
