The short version

At a glance

  1. Sign in to admin.google.com.
  2. Go to Menu → Security → Access and data control → API controls and click Manage App Access.
  3. Under Configured apps, click Configure new app and search for the Nova Guide client ID.
  4. Select Nova Guide, keep the top-level organizational unit (or pick specific OUs), and click Continue.
  5. Under Access to Google data, choose Specific Google data and add the seven scopes below. Click Continue → Finish.

Already have a student request? On the Admin console home page, open App access control → Review apps, point to Nova Guide, and click Configure access. Then pick up at step 4.

The detailed version

Step by step in the Google Admin console

  1. Open App Access Go to admin.google.com, open Menu → Security → Access and data control → API controls, and click Manage App Access. You'll see Configured apps (apps that already have a setting) and Accessed apps (apps users have signed in to).
  2. Find Nova Guide by client ID Under Configured apps, click Configure new app, paste the client ID above, click Search, and click Nova Guide in the results.
  3. Choose who it applies to For Scope, leave the top-level organizational unit selected to cover every student who chooses to use Nova Guide. This is the simplest option, approved once.
    • Prefer to start smaller? Click Select org units → Include organizations, tick the OUs you want (up to 10 at a time), and click Select. You can widen it later.
    Then click Continue.
  4. Choose the access level Under Access to Google data, choose Specific Google data and add the seven scopes, or choose Trusted. See which access level below. Click Continue.
  5. Review and finish Review the summary and click Finish. If you started from a student request, Google also shows a Confirm parental consent notice and offers to notify the students who asked. Changes usually apply within minutes, but can take up to 24 hours to reach every user.

Check

Verify the approval worked

  1. Ask a student to open Nova Guide → Settings → Accounts & Calendars → Connect Google Classroom, or use the connect prompt on the Assignments tab.
  2. They should reach Google's consent screen listing the read-only scopes, with no "unverified app" warning. They approve, and their assignments appear.
  3. Google Calendar is connected separately from the same Settings screen.

Still blocked? See troubleshooting.

Education editions

Under-18 students and parental consent

  • In Google Workspace for Education, users designated as under 18 are blocked from every third-party app an admin hasn't configured. That's why students see "Access blocked" until you approve.
  • Under Choose settings for unconfigured apps, you can set different rules for users over and under 18, and choose whether under-18 users can request access to apps for themselves.
  • Google states that your organization is responsible for obtaining parental consent, if required by applicable law, before allowing users under 18 to access third-party apps. Nova Guide is designed for students 14 and older and collects no personal information on any server, but the consent decision is your district's.

Google verification and your approval are different things. Google verified Nova Guide on October 2, 2026, so students see no "unverified app" warning. But on a restricted domain, your approval is still what allows students to connect at all.

Later on

Changing or revoking access

  • For an organizational unit: in API controls → Manage App Access, point to Nova Guide under Configured apps and click Change access. Or open the app, go to Access to Google data, pick the OU, and set it to Blocked. Reads stop for students in that OU.
  • Per student: the student can disconnect in Nova Guide (Settings → Accounts & Calendars, separately for Classroom and Calendar) or remove access at myaccount.google.com.
  • The data: revoking never changes Classroom or Calendar, because the app never wrote to them. Disconnecting deletes the imported copy from the iPhone. A short history of assignments the student marked completed stays on their phone, as their own record, until they delete the app. It's never transmitted.
