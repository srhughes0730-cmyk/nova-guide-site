Who it's for

Made for busy, overloaded students

Nova Guide is designed for U.S. high-school and college students — especially anyone who struggles with time management, prioritization, or simply getting started. It meets you where you are and helps you take the next small step.

Your data stays on your iPhone. Nova Guide has no accounts and no servers. Google Classroom and Calendar connections are optional and read-only: your assignments and events go straight from Google to your device to build your plan, and are never sent to us.

Private by design. The plan quietly gets more realistic as it learns how long your work actually takes, and on supported iPhones it can use Apple's on-device intelligence to help word suggested steps and study tips. All of that happens on your device — nothing is sent to us or any outside AI service. See our Privacy Policy.

Nova Guide is an organizational and educational tool. It is not a medical, diagnostic, or therapeutic product, and it does not promise specific outcomes.
